## Fixed experiment and stronger family guard

Twelve logistic/histogram candidates compare statistics27 controls, context36 and context34
without ports, each under natural or connection/class-balanced training weights. Parameters,
train-only preprocessing, calibration-only UID-max threshold selection and validation ranking
otherwise follow the previous statistical protocol. All training/prediction uses one thread,
seed42; a dummy and two selected-model repeats complete fifteen fits. One-hour/1.5 GiB sampled
screens remain. Warm-up rows stay present and all linkage exclusions stay unchanged.

Binary precision >=98%, FPR <=.5%, recall >=80% and at least25 true-positive connections remain
the initial screens. Additionally, require >=25 validation connections and >=80% recall for
**each C&C, DDoS and horizontal-scan family** before qualifying this study's candidate. This
guard is registered before fitting, not applied retrospectively to alter the previous results.
Other absent/unsupported attack families remain unassessed. Publish conditional family intervals
and support; one capture/family and dependent UIDs do not provide independent event uncertainty.

Validation still chooses the diagnostic. If no candidate passes both binary and family screens,
retain the best diagnostic and report rejection rather than adjusting validation thresholds.
Repeat both scores/decisions at the frozen calibration threshold; require 1e-7 maximum score
difference and zero decision differences. Record whether repeat calibration thresholds match.
Saved/reloaded probes must agree exactly. Multiple seeds, devices and sources remain future work.

initialization sequence, watermark, warm-up/late/cache metadata, raw addresses, UID, labels or
source/capture IDs. Context history is computed from all observed starts, not just labeled
ones. Verify input bytes, complete joins, prefix clocks and each registered logical digest
before fitting. Report float64/float32 pattern diagnostics and the selected model's existing
aggregate error bins; no individual rows/scores are published.

and a trusted frozen model hash must be checked before loading joblib. Payloads may include
only the fitted bundle, synthetic portability probes and its public freeze metadata. No real
feature/UID/endpoint rows or public weights are exported. This is private research retention,
not public-model licensing clearance or deployment authorization.
